Who is J.LINK TEXTILE?

J.LINK TEXTILE (Jiangling Digital Printing) is a wide-width digital fabric printing factory located in China Textile City, Keqiao District, Shaoxing, Zhejiang, China. We specialize in digital printing services for curtains, wallcoverings and home textiles for B2B buyers and OEM clients.

Equipped with imported high-speed digital printing machines, we cover printing widths of 200 / 220 / 240 / 260 / 280 / 320CM — six standard widths, making us one of the few factories capable of full-width production. We offer disperse printing, polyester printing and wide-width digital printing — three core processes — and recommend the most suitable process based on your fabric type.

MOQ 1 meter, free sampling (1-2 designs for first orders). Sample fee is deductible from bulk orders — we handle both small and large orders, and respond quickly to new-product trials, e-commerce restocking and cross-border fast replenishment.

Located in China Textile City — the world's largest textile distribution hub — we enjoy convenient raw-material sourcing and a complete supply chain, delivering efficient, stable and flexible OEM service.

Our Journey

2018 · Start

Established the factory in China Textile City, Keqiao, focusing on digital printing OEM, initially serving local curtain / home textile clients.

2019-2020 · Expansion

Introduced 320CM wide-width digital printing equipment, becoming one of the few Keqiao factories capable of ultra-wide production. Client base expanded nationwide.

2021-2022 · Process upgrade

Built disperse + polyester dual-process lines covering polyester / synthetic / blends. Adopted ICC color management, ΔE < 1.5.

2023-2024 · Systematized QC

Established three-stage QC (pre-print / in-line / post-print) and partnered with SGS and the National Textile Testing Center. Achieved Oeko-Tex environmental certification.

2025-2026 · Digital transformation

Launched the official website (bu6789.com) as an online acquisition channel. Upgraded to three parallel processes (disperse / polyester / wide-width digital); widths covering six standards 200/220/240/260/280/320CM; MOQ lowered to 1m with free sampling. Clients exceeded 500+, monthly capacity surpassed 600,000 meters. Began cross-border e-commerce and digital brand operations.
