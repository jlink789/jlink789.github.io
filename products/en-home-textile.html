FAQ

Q: Does bedding printing make the fabric stiff?

No. We have a dedicated softening finish; polyester-printed fabric stays skin-friendly and soft, and disperse-printed polyester fabric is not stiff either. Hand feel is a mandatory outgoing inspection item.

Q: Is there a minimum order for home textile printing?

MOQ 1m, unlimited quantity per design. For home textile brands needing multiple designs, we support combined multi-design orders — each design from 1m, as long as the total meets the MOQ. First orders get 1-2 designs free sampling.

Q: Any special requirements for export home textiles?

Export to EU/US requires Oeko-Tex or REACH compliance; our imported inks are already certified. Disperse printing suits mainstream Western markets (synthetic home textiles); polyester printing suits bedding, cushions, tablecloths and similar categories.
