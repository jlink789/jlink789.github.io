FAQ

Q: What is the benefit of 200-320CM wide wallcovering?

Standard residential ceiling heights are 2.6-3.2m. Full-width coverage means one piece per wall with no horizontal seams — eliminating the aesthetic issue of join lines. MOQ 1m, free sampling, flexible width choice. This is the core selling point of premium seamless wallcovering.

Q: Can large commercial orders keep batch-to-batch color consistency?

Yes. Same batch uses unified ink mixing and process parameters, with intra-batch ΔE ≤ 1.5. Across batches we keep reference samples for color matching to ensure consistency. We recommend placing commercial orders in one go to avoid cross-batch issues.

Q: Does printed wallcovering need further treatment?

We offer optional water-, stain- and flame-retardant finishing. Standard wallcovering leaves the factory after steaming/fixation and washing/setting and can be installed directly.
