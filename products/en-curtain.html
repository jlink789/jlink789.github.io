FAQ

Q: What are the advantages of curtain digital printing vs traditional printing?

No plate-making, 16.7 million colors, MOQ 1m, fast 5-7 day lead time, and zero-cost easy pattern changes — ideal for customized curtains and small-batch orders.

Q: How does 200-320CM width benefit curtains?

Most curtains are 2.5-2.8m tall. Multiple width options let one piece of fabric yield one curtain with no vertical seams — far better aesthetics.

Q: Does printing reduce the blackout effect of blackout curtains?

No. We use surface printing that does not damage the blackout layer. The printed area keeps the same blackout rate as the base fabric — measured at 90%-100% blackout.
