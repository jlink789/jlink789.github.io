Keqiao (Shaoxing) is home to China's Light Textile City, one of the world's largest textile hubs. Sourcing curtain printing here means efficiency and cost benefits from a clustered supply chain — fabric, printing, dyeing, finishing and logistics all nearby, with leading small-batch fast-response capability.

Core Cluster Advantages

The biggest strength of Keqiao curtain printing is nearby matching. A curtain goes from grey cloth to finished product through fabric selection, printing, steaming, washing and finishing. In Keqiao these steps sit within a few kilometers:

  • Fabric hub: polyester, synthetic, blend and chenille for curtains all in one place, fast sampling
  • Complete process: disperse printing, heat transfer and wide-format digital printing done nearby, no out-of-town subcontracting
  • Full finishing: steaming, washing, setting and calendering mature, lead time controllable
  • Export logistics: consolidated shipping to Ningbo and Shanghai ports, easy cross-border delivery

Why Small-Batch Fast Response Matters

Curtain orders are increasingly fragmented: e-commerce replenishment, designer private molds, hotel model rooms — single designs often only dozens to hundreds of meters. Traditional dyeing needs high minimums and slow screens. Wide-format digital printing needs no screens, 1-meter minimum with free sampling, perfect for fragmented demand.

  • Light trial: sample first, then bulk, low risk
  • Fast reorder: designs archived digitally, reprint on demand
  • Multi-design: mix designs in one order at no extra cost
