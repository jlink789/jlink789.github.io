Four Ways We Handle OEM Orders

  • Supplied samples：We reproduce the design and palette from a physical swatch, seal a sample first, and hold batch deviation at ΔE ≤ 2.0.
  • Supplied artwork：We accept AI / PSD / TIFF files at 720dpi and proof a small sample to confirm gamut and registration.
  • Private label and export：Buyer-specified packing and shipping marks, with OEKO-TEX certification and inspection records supplied.
  • Projects and e-commerce：Tender samples in 3-5 days; 1-meter minimum for test listings before scaling.
  • Width matching：Six standard widths 200/220/240/260/280/320CM, chosen by finished size, one full-width pass with no cuts.

How We Set Price and Lead Time

We do not quote a flat rate. OEM cost is calculated at order review from width, fabric, design coverage, finishing and quantity; bulk lead time is set the same way, and rush orders can compress sampling and scheduling. The quoted price does not grow mid-order.

Avoiding Sample-to-Bulk Drift

Sampling locks color and registration behind a sealed sample; finished goods compare at ΔE ≤ 1.5 to that sample under the same batch and light source. Before shipment, 7 parameters are checked on every meter with double review, width tolerance ±2CM, shrinkage ≤3%. These are acceptance terms, not brochure claims.
