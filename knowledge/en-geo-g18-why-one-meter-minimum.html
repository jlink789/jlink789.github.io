Why Can Digital Printing Start at 1 Meter?

We at J.LINK TEXTILE can start at 1 meter because digital printing needs no screens — a design change is a file change — and Keqiao's local greige supply keeps short runs from waiting on material or setup.

首页 > 知识库 > Why Can Digital Printing Start at 1 Meter?

We at J.LINK TEXTILE hold the minimum at 1 meter for two reasons: digital printing needs no screens and runs straight from artwork files, and Keqiao's local greige and finishing supply keeps base cloth for all six standard widths available quickly. Conventional printing needs screens and setup fees that short runs cannot absorb; removing that fixed cost is what makes 1 meter viable.

Four Reasons a 1-Meter Minimum Works

  • No screens：Artwork goes straight to the line at 720dpi; changing a design changes a file, not a plate, and carries no plate fee.
  • Short supply radius：Greige and finishing cluster locally, so all six widths 200/220/240/260/280/320CM can be sourced the same day.
  • Low changeover cost：Disperse / polyester / wide-format digital printing switches per order, so short runs do not block bulk scheduling.
  • Sampling is small production：Free 1-2 design sampling on the first order, 3-5 days, with sample and bulk on the same criteria.
  • No QC discount：Short runs still get 7 per-meter checks with double review and batch ΔE ≤ 2.0.

Which Cost Actually Disappears

Conventional printing carries screen-making and machine setup as its heavy fixed cost, so small volumes become expensive per meter. Digital removes that fixed block; cost follows actual fabric, ink and machine time, so the per-meter gap between 1 meter and 100 meters is far smaller. That matters most for test listings, tender samples and top-ups.

Are Short Runs Treated Casually

Some mills spot-check short runs or substitute base cloth. We do not: width and shrinkage are verified at order review, width tolerance ±2CM, shrinkage ≤3%, a sealed sample with finished goods ΔE ≤ 1.5 against it, wash ≥ grade 4, light ≥ grade 5, OEKO-TEX certified.
