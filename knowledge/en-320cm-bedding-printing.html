320CM Wide-Format Bedding Printing: One-Piece Bedspreads

Oversized bedspreads and sheets suffer from mismatched repeats when split across narrow widths. J.LINK offers 200-320CM standards up to 320CM, printing in one seamless piece.

首页 > 知识库 > 320CM Wide-Format Bedding Printing: One-Piece Bedspreads

Oversized bedspreads and enlarged sheets are increasingly popular, but traditional narrow machines must split the fabric and sew it, causing mismatched repeats and broken patterns on the bed. 320CM wide-format digital printing lets oversized bedding form in one piece, solving this at the root.

Why Oversized Bedding Needs 320CM

Bedspreads and enlarged sheets often exceed 2.4m in width; standard 1.5-1.8m machines cannot cover them at once and must split. Splitting causes two problems: hard-to-align seams and broken large motifs. 320CM wide-format direct printing forms the bedding seamlessly across the width, with a continuous pattern.
