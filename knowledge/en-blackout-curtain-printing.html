Blackout Curtain Printing: Combining Blackout Base with Print

Blackout curtain printing puts color on a blackout base. J.LINK uses wide-format digital printing + heat transfer, seamless, continuous shading, 1-meter minimum.

首页 > 知识库 > Blackout Curtain Printing: Combining Blackout Base with Print

Blackout curtains ask for more than ordinary ones: good color and real shading. The core of blackout curtain printing is to add color on a blackout base while keeping the shading layer continuous and unbroken.

Blackout Curtain Structure

A blackout curtain is usually "blackout base + color layer". The base is silver-coated, white-coated or densely woven polyester for blocking light; the color layer is added by printing. The key is not letting printing break shading continuity.

  • Coated fabric: great shading, watch print temperature to protect coat
  • Dense weave: shading by weave, freer printing
  • Composite base: shading layer at back, front printing unaffected

Why Wide-Format Full Width

Blackout curtains fear seam light-leak most. Wide-format digital printing direct jets full width, keeping shading uncut and color continuous:

Process Selection

  • Polyester blackout base: heat transfer / disperse, color + sun
  • Coat-sensitive fabric: lower-temp heat transfer or direct print, protect coat

Notes
