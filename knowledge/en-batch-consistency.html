Sampling-to-Bulk Consistency in Digital Printing

Bulk drifting from sample is the top complaint. J.LINK controls batch consistency to ΔE<1.5 via sealed sample, ICC curves and standard fixing.

首页 > 知识库 > Sampling-to-Bulk Consistency in Digital Printing

Bulk drifting from the approved sample is the most common complaint in printing OEM. Consistency is not by eye but by system: sealed sample, color curves and standard fixing.

Three-Step Control

1. Sealed sample: first piece or supplied sample is sealed as the only bulk standard

2. Curve lock: independent ICC curve per line controls machine drift

3. Fixed finishing: standardized steaming, washing, finishing stops color shift

常见问题

Bulk differs from sample?

Usually no sealed sample or machine drift. J.LINK uses sealed sample, ICC lock and standard fixing for batch ΔE<1.5.

What is a sealed sample?

The approved first piece or supplied sample is sealed as the only bulk reference, removing subjective judgment.

Stable across reorders?

Yes: sealed sample plus curves plus fixing keep brand reorders and project runs consistent.
