Keqiao (Shaoxing) is home to China's Light Textile City and a major hub for wallcovering and soft-furnishing fabrics. Sourcing wallcovering printing here means a clustered supply chain — from greige fabric and printing to finishing and export logistics — plus the fast-response capability that only an industrial cluster can deliver.

Core Advantages of Sourcing in Keqiao

Keqiao has a complete textile chain. The polyester and synthetic base cloth used for wallcoverings is sourced locally; after printing, finishing such as sizing, setting and lamination is done nearby, with no cross-province transit. This shortens the sampling-to-bulk path and makes switching between designs fast.

For brands and traders, the density of Keqiao digital printing factories brings transparent pricing, elastic capacity and quick replenishment: a trial design can be re-sampled the same day, and a hit SKU can be reordered from nearby capacity.

How Fast Response Works

Wide-format digital printing needs no screens; once the design file is confirmed it goes on machine, enabling a trial-to-reorder rhythm. J.LINK TEXTILE, a Keqiao digital printing factory, supports 1-meter minimum and free sampling, with a standard 5-7 working-day lead time, fit for e-commerce, hotel projects and cross-border fast response.

Fast response also means stable: sealed samples and color curves hold batch delta E under 1.5, so sample equals bulk across reorders. Heat-transfer (sublimation) support is also mature locally.

QCan one wall be printed as a single drop?

AYes — that is the point of wide format. 200 to 320CM covered in six standard widths; at a typical 2.7-3 m ceiling, 320CM lets one wall run as a single sheet with no seam on the wall.

QDo non-woven, hot-melt and cross-base backings all run on your line?

AAll three. Non-woven and hot-melt run disperse printing; coated bases get a different pre-treatment. Because absorbency and grammage differ, we test a short run before printing the full roll.

QWill you take a single-wall order of just a dozen meters?

AYes. 1-meter minimum with no plate-making; sampling in 1-2 days, bulk in 5-7 working days.
