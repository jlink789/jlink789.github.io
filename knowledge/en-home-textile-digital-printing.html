What Is Home-Textile Digital Printing? Bedding, Cushion & Tablecloth Customization

Home-textile digital printing jets onto bedding, cushions and tablecloths with grade 4+ fastness and Oeko-Tex compliance, 1-meter minimum.

Home-textile digital printing jets patterns onto bedding, cushions, tablecloths and throws. It supports small-batch personalization with fine color and high fastness, Oeko-Tex compliant, fit for home brands and cross-border e-commerce.

Why Home Textiles Fit Digital

Home textiles refresh patterns fast with flexible volume. Digital needs no screens, 1-meter minimum, responds quickly to new items and replenishment. 8 inks give 16.7M colors with smooth gradients.

QWhich process for bed sheets, and will it fade after washing?

ABed sheet printing runs on disperse printing or heat transfer, on polyester and poly-cotton blends. Colour fastness grade 4+, wash and light resistant, OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified.

QHow do widths map to 200×230 and 245×250 sheet sizes?

AWe work back from the finished size: 240CM for 200×230, 260CM for 245×250, 280/320CM for oversized and hotel specs — printed in one piece, so hemming needs no allowance.

QIs a few hundred meters per pattern too small to be economical?

ADigital printing needs no plates and starts at 1 meter. The gap between 300 m and 1,500 m sits in fabric and process, not in plate cost. Sample free first, then set quantity.
