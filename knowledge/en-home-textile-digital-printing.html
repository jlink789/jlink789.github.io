What Is Home-Textile Digital Printing? Bedding, Cushion & Tablecloth Customization

Home-textile digital printing jets onto bedding, cushions and tablecloths with grade 4+ fastness and Oeko-Tex compliance, 1-meter minimum.

Home-textile digital printing jets patterns onto bedding, cushions, tablecloths and throws. It supports small-batch personalization with fine color and high fastness, Oeko-Tex compliant, fit for home brands and cross-border e-commerce.

Why Home Textiles Fit Digital

Home textiles refresh patterns fast with flexible volume. Digital needs no screens, 1-meter minimum, responds quickly to new items and replenishment. 8 inks give 16.7M colors with smooth gradients.
