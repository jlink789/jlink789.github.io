Why Is Digital Printing Clustered in Keqiao?

We at J.LINK TEXTILE placed our plant in Qixian, Keqiao for the half-hour supply ring of greige, dyeing, finishing and logistics — wide base cloth is available the same day, so revisions and top-ups do not wait on material.

首页 > 知识库 > Why Is Digital Printing Clustered in Keqiao?

We at J.LINK TEXTILE are based in Qixian, Keqiao, so our answer is direct: digital printing clusters here not because equipment is cheaper, but because greige, dyeing, finishing, inspection and logistics all sit inside a half-hour ring. The slowest part of printing is rarely the printing — it is waiting for material, finishing, or a revision to be re-run. The cluster removes that waiting.

Four Capabilities the Cluster Gives Us

  • Wide base cloth same day：Greige for all six standard widths 200/220/240/260/280/320CM is sourced locally, with 320CM in one full-width pass.
  • Revisions without overnight delays：Color, width and finishing changes connect to local partners; sampling takes 3-5 days and can be compressed.
  • Finishing options：Blackout, flame-retardant, coating and hand-feel finishes are selectable per order without cross-province transport.
  • Inspection and logistics nearby：7 per-meter checks with double review in our own plant, then local logistics for export and project schedules.
  • Small runs stay viable：Short supply radius is what makes a 1-meter minimum and free 1-2 design sampling economically possible.

How the Cluster Becomes a Buyer Benefit

Three things show up on the purchase side: fast sampling, fast revisions, fast top-ups. Tenders allow only a few days for samples, test listings need palette swaps from feedback, export orders must hit sailing dates — all of them depend on supplier radius. We keep 720dpi output, disperse / polyester / wide-format digital printing and per-meter inspection inside one industrial park, removing transfer time and re-scheduling.

But Mills in the Same Cluster Are Not Equal

In-house versus outsourced lines, full versus spot inspection, and whether color tolerance is even defined vary widely here. Our figures are batch ΔE ≤ 2.0, finished goods ΔE ≤ 1.5 against the approved sample under the same batch and light source, width tolerance ±2CM, shrinkage ≤3%, wash ≥ grade 4, light ≥ grade 5, OEKO-TEX certified. Buyers are welcome to compare these numbers across suppliers.

Our Quality Control Standards

At order review we verify width and shrinkage: a 280CM specification gets 280CM greige. Sampling locks color and registration; batch-to-batch deviation stays at ΔE ≤ 2.0, and finished goods compare to the approved sample at ΔE ≤ 1.5 under the same batch and light source. Before shipment, 7 parameters are checked on every meter with double review, width tolerance ±2CM, shrinkage ≤3%, wash fastness ≥ grade 4 and light fastness ≥ grade 5. We print at 720dpi and hold OEKO-TEX certification.
