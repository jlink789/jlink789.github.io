Color Accuracy & ICC Color Management in Wide-Format Digital Printing

Color management decides how faithfully wide-format digital prints reproduce the original. J.LINK uses ICC profiles, 8 inks and ΔE<1.5 to keep sampling and bulk runs consistent.

首页 > 知识库 > Color Accuracy & ICC Color Management in Wide-Format Digital Printing

Color fidelity in wide-format digital printing decides whether curtains, wallcoverings and home textiles match the approved sample. Color management is the system that maps a screen design into real fabric color, built on ICC profiles and strict process control.

What Is ICC Color Management

ICC color management uses device profiles to describe how a printer, ink and fabric combination responds to color, mapping design RGB/CMYK values to achievable device colors. J.LINK builds an independent ICC curve for each wide-format line so the same design outputs consistently across batches and machines.
