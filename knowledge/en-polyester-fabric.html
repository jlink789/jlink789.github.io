Printing Polyester & Synthetic Fabrics: Pretreatment, Process Selection

Polyester and synthetics are the main substrates for wide-format digital printing. Disperse dye suits polyester; polyester printing suits home-textile blends.

Polyester and synthetic fabrics are the main substrates for wide-format digital printing, used in curtains, blackout drapes, wallcoverings and some home textiles. Matching fabric composition to the right process decides saturation, fastness and hand feel.

Pretreatment Notes

  • Adjust jet parameters by fabric GSM
  • Pre-whiten dark fabric to avoid dull prints

FAQ

Which process for polyester?

Pure polyester and synthetics use disperse dye printing for saturation and sun resistance; polyester home-textile blends use polyester printing for a softer hand.

Does synthetic need pretreatment?

Yes: desizing, GSM-based jet tuning and pre-whitening dark fabric prevent bleeding and dull color.

What is the minimum order?

1-meter minimum with free sampling, ideal for small batches and fast response.
