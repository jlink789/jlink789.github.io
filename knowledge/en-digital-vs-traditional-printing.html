Digital vs Traditional Printing: Cost, Lead Time & Quality Compared

Digital printing needs no screens, 1-meter minimum, 5-7 day delivery; traditional needs screens, high minimums, longer lead time. Choose by order profile.

首页 > 知识库 > Digital vs Traditional Printing: Cost, Lead Time & Quality Compared

The core difference between digital and traditional printing (rotary, flat-screen) is screening. Digital jets from a file with no screens; traditional prints after making screens. This decides minimums, lead time, design count and small-batch cost.

Spec Comparison

Cost Structure

Traditional printing amortizes screen cost per meter; larger runs are cheaper but small runs are uneconomical. Digital has no screen fee, 1-meter minimum, friendly for small batches; per-meter cost at huge volume is slightly higher but saves screens and minimums.

Lead Time & Fast Response

Digital delivers in 5-7 days, sampling 1-2 days, fit for e-commerce replenishment and new-product trials. Traditional depends on screens, 15-30 days, fit for large stable runs.

Quality & Fastness

Both reach grade 4+ fastness. Digital achieves high fidelity via ICC management; traditional is limited by color stations. Wide-format digital also prints seamless, hard for traditional narrow machines.

Selection

  • Multi-design, small batch, fast response, trial → digital
  • Large volume, single design, cost-first → traditional
  • Large-surface full-width → wide-format digital (seamless)

常见问题

Is digital printing more expensive?

For small batches digital is cheaper (no screens). For huge single-design runs traditional per-meter cost is lower. Choose by order.

Why is digital lead time shorter?

No screens; jets from file, 5-7 days. Traditional needs screens first, 15-30 days overall.

What fastness can digital reach?

Grade 4+, with high fidelity via ICC management, same level as traditional.

Which is best for small batches?

Digital: 1-meter minimum, free sampling, no screen barrier.

Is seamless only possible with digital?

Wide-format digital prints in one pass up to 320CM; traditional narrow machines need joining.
