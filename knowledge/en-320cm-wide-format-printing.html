What Is 320CM Wide-Format Digital Printing? Six Standard Widths, Seamless

J.LINK TEXTILE covers six standard widths up to 320CM, seamless in one piece, built for curtains, wallcoverings and large-surface products.

首页 > 知识库 > What Is 320CM Wide-Format Digital Printing? Six Standard Widths, Seamless

320CM wide-format digital printing uses wide industrial printers to jet patterns onto fabric up to 320CM wide in a single piece. J.LINK TEXTILE covers six standard widths 200/220/240/260/280/320CM, among the few factories that stably process full widths.

Six Standard Widths

  • 200CM: regular curtains, small bedding items
  • 220CM / 240CM: standard curtains, sheers
  • 260CM / 280CM: large-window curtains, wide wallcoverings
  • 320CM: ultra-wide curtains, seamless wallcoverings, backdrops

Six standards let customers pick the most economical width per finished size, reducing waste and seams.

Key Advantage: Seamless

320CM seamless printing means curtains and wallcoverings run in one piece, with no mismatch and no seam defects. For high-end soft furnishings, visual continuity defines the grade. Narrow machines need joining; wide-format prints in one pass.

FAQ

Must 320CM printing be exactly 320CM?

No. Six widths 200-320CM are available; pick the best fit per finished size to avoid waste.

Why is seamless better than joined?

One-piece printing has no mismatch or seam, giving large curtains and wallcoverings higher visual continuity and grade.

What products fit 320CM?

Full-height curtains, blackout drapes, seamless wallcoverings, hotel textiles, large backdrops.

Can bulk 320CM be stable?

Yes. 8+ lines, 600k+ meters monthly, thousand-meter orders stable.
