We at J.LINK TEXTILE sample curtain digital printing in 3-5 days, with rush orders compressible; the first order includes free sampling of 1-2 designs and a 1-meter minimum. That turnaround comes from screenless, direct-from-file output plus Keqiao's local greige and finishing supply — base cloth for all six standard widths is available the same day.

What Happens Inside the Sampling Window

  • Order review：We check artwork, finished width, fabric and shrinkage, then allocate one of six widths 200/220/240/260/280/320CM.
  • Proofing：720dpi output, with disperse / polyester / wide-format digital printing selected by fabric.
  • Approval and sealing：Once color, registration and hand feel are approved, the sample is sealed as the bulk acceptance reference.
  • Supporting material：Color cards and test criteria available — tender samples follow the same route.
  • Rush：Tender or sailing-date pressure can compress scheduling; the node is fixed at order review.

What Changes the Sampling Time

Whether artwork needs color separation, whether a palette must be reproduced from a physical swatch, whether special finishing is required (blackout, flame retardant, coating), and how many designs are proofed at once. For a first order, sending artwork, a target color reference or physical swatch, and finished width together usually keeps it within 3-5 days.

The Goal Is a Sealed Sample, Not a Swatch

We lock color and registration at proofing and seal the sample, hold batch ΔE ≤ 2.0 and finished goods ΔE ≤ 1.5 against the approved sample under the same batch and light source, and re-run repeat orders against the same reference. Bulk lead time is set at order review by design complexity and width, with 7 per-meter checks and double review before shipment.
