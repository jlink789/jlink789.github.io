We at J.LINK TEXTILE start at 1 meter, with free sampling of 1-2 designs on the first order. There are no screen charges and no volume threshold. This matters to the 500+ B2B buyers and OEM clients we serve: e-commerce tests, hotel model rooms, cross-border launches and tender samples often need only a few to a few dozen meters, with no reason to hold excess stock.

Why a 1-Meter Minimum Works for Us

Conventional printing needs screens, and screen plus setup costs make small runs uneconomic. We use wide-format digital printing: artwork goes straight to the line, no screens, and changing a design means changing a file and transfer paper. Cost per meter does not spike on short runs, so 1 meter is viable and multiple designs can be scheduled together.

Are Small Runs Treated Differently?

No. Small orders follow the same QC as bulk: 720dpi printing, batch-to-batch deviation ΔE ≤ 2.0, finished goods ΔE ≤ 1.5 against the approved sample under the same batch and light source, width tolerance ±2CM and shrinkage ≤3%. Before release, 7 parameters are checked on every meter — color difference, pattern deviation, gsm, width, color fastness, shrinkage and surface defects — with double review, not spot checks. A short run is still one full-width pass with no cuts.

Four Good Fits

1. E-commerce testing — list small, then replenish fast against a sealed sample.

2. Hotels and model rooms — precise yardage per room, no surplus inventory.

3. Cross-border launches — trial one design in several markets.

4. Project tenders — submit a full-width sample curtain before bulk scheduling.

What to Prepare

Artwork files (higher resolution gives better fidelity), fabric or finished specification, target width, application and deadline.
