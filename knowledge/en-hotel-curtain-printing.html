Hotel & Project Curtain Printing: Batch Consistency, Lead Time, Acceptance

Hotel project curtains need one color across a thousand rooms. J.LINK uses sealed sample + ICC curves for batch consistency, wide-format digital printing 5-7 day lead time, seamless.

首页 > 知识库 > Hotel & Project Curtain Printing: Batch Consistency, Lead Time, Acceptance

Curtains for hotels, model rooms and fitted projects are hard not in "looking good" but in "being consistent" — one design must keep one color and one match across dozens or thousands of rooms. Batch consistency and lead-time acceptance are the two gates of project orders.

Core Project Pain Points

  • Consistency: different batches and floors must match, no visible difference
  • Lead time: opening date is fixed; one day late is a loss
  • Acceptance: buyer checks against sealed sample; over-tolerance means whole batch return

Wide-format digital printing resolves these by data and system, not by master's feel.
