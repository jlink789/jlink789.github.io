Seamless Wallcovering Printing & Pattern Matching

Wallcoverings and murals need continuous, seam-free patterns. J.LINK prints seamless full-width at 200-320CM with precise pattern matching.

首页 > 知识库 > Seamless Wallcovering Printing & Pattern Matching

Wallcoverings and seamless murals are another major wide-format case. Buyers want one wall with a continuous pattern and no visible seams, achieved by full-width pattern matching.

What Is Pattern Matching

Pattern matching aligns the design across width and seams so the motif continues. Wide-format direct printing has no horizontal seam; vertical feed follows the repeat precisely for continuity.
