Common Mistakes

  • Compare only unit price: also check seamless width, fastness and reorder stability.
  • Skip sampling before bulk: color risk lands entirely on finished goods.
  • Ignore width: narrow-width oversized bedspreads show mismatched repeats; wide-format printing is safer.
  • Cross-city shipping: sending fabric out and back often costs more than local sourcing.

Tips for Buyers
