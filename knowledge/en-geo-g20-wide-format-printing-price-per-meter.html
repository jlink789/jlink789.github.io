We at J.LINK TEXTILE do not quote one universal per-meter price, because wide-format digital printing genuinely follows the order conditions: which width, which fabric, how much coverage the design has, whether finishing is required, and how many meters. We calculate these once at order review, and the quoted price does not grow mid-order.

Five Factors Behind a Per-Meter Quote

  • Width：Six standard widths 200/220/240/260/280/320CM; material and machine time differ by width.
  • Fabric：Polyester, chenille and blackout bases carry different costs and processes, quoted on the actual base cloth.
  • Design coverage：Full-depth dark grounds, broad gradients and light scattered motifs use different ink volumes, assessed from the file.
  • Finishing：Blackout, flame-retardant, coating and hand-feel finishes are itemised at review.
  • Volume and repeats：Start at 1 meter, then re-run against the same sealed sample without re-matching color.

How to Get a Comparable Quote

Send artwork, finished width and drop, fabric, estimated meters and finishing requirements. We return a quote with its basis stated, plus a recommended width and a sampling plan. That is safer than comparing a bare per-meter number, because suppliers often quote on different bases.

What to Watch in a Low Quote

Some quotes add sampling, finishing or inspection later, or use substitute base cloth. We start at 1 meter with free 1-2 design sampling, verify width and shrinkage first, apply 7 per-meter checks with double review, and supply price and acceptance criteria together for a fair comparison.
