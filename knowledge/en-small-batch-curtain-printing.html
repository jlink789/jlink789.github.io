Curtain orders are shifting from large long-cycle runs to small fast-response batches. E-commerce tests, hotel model rooms, cross-border new products and custom soft furnishings often need only dozens or a few meters to start. Whether small-batch curtain printing works well hinges on 1-meter minimum, free sampling, wide-format seamless output and fast replenishment.

Who Needs Small-Batch Curtain Printing

  • E-commerce testing: small batch to test best-sellers, then quick reorders.
  • Hotel/model rooms: precise yardage per room, no large inventory.
  • Cross-border new products: small trials across market designs.
  • Custom soft furnishings: designer projects, small and varied.
  • Fast replenishment: urgent reorders delivered in 5-7 days.

Meaning of 1-Meter Minimum and Free Sampling

Traditional printing needs screens, high minimums and costly sampling — poor for small runs. Digital printing needs no screens; J.LINK supports 1-meter minimum and free sampling: sample in 1-2 days to confirm color and match, seal, then bulk, minimizing risk.

Small batch is not compromise. Even a few meters of floor or blackout drape should be seamless. J.LINK offers six standard widths 200/220/240/260/280/320CM up to 320CM; wide-format direct printing makes small curtains one-piece with precise match.
