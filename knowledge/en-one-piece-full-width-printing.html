What Does One-Piece Full-Width Printing Mean?

For us at J.LINK TEXTILE, one-piece full-width output means printing the entire required width in a single pass across our 200-320CM standard widths, with continuous patterns and no cut segments.

首页 > 知识库 > What Does One-Piece Full-Width Printing Mean?

We at J.LINK TEXTILE define "one-piece full-width output, no cuts" as printing the entire width a finished product needs in a single pass at the selected standard width. The pattern stays continuous from edge to edge, and the finished drape or wall surface is one uninterrupted piece rather than a picture split into sections and aligned afterwards. This is the practical difference a wide-format line makes, and it is our default for hotel project drapes, mural wallcoverings and paired curtains in large apartments.

Why B2B Buyers Care

  • Smoother project sign-off: lobby and tall drapes read as one complete image with no visual break.
  • Fewer steps: no downstream alignment of separate printed sections, so shorter lead times and less waste.
  • Lower rework risk: we verify width and shrinkage at order review; width tolerance ±2CM, shrinkage ≤3%.
  • Design freedom: large repeats, gradients and continuous lines render intact at 720dpi.

How We Keep It Consistent

Three stages do the work: order review (derive the width from finished drop, fullness ratio and seam allowance, then pick the closest of six tiers), sampling (1-meter minimum, free sampling of 1-2 designs on the first order, locking color and registration), and release (7 checks on every meter plus double review: color difference, pattern deviation, gsm, width, color fastness, shrinkage, surface defects). Batch-to-batch deviation holds at ΔE ≤ 2.0; finished goods compare to the approved sample at ΔE ≤ 1.5 under the same batch and light source.

Orders That Benefit Most

Floor-length and blackout curtains, hotel lobby drapes, paired curtains, extra-wide and mural wallcoverings, and wide bedding. We serve 500+ B2B buyers and OEM clients, and these categories almost always run one-piece. Smaller items such as cushions and table linen are more economical on the 200CM tier.

FAQ

What does one-piece full-width output mean?

For us at J.LINK TEXTILE it means printing the entire required width in a single pass across our 200-320CM standard widths, with continuous patterns and no cut segments.

Does full width require 320CM?

We choose by finished size: tall drapes and extra-wide murals often use 280/320CM, standard curtains work on 240/260CM, and all six tiers run one-piece.

How is registration controlled?

We lock color and registration at sampling, hold batch deviation at ΔE ≤ 2.0 and finished goods at ΔE ≤ 1.5, with 7 per-meter checks and double review.

Can I sample a one-piece order?

Yes. We start at 1 meter with free sampling of 1-2 designs on the first order, normally 3-5 days, so project tenders can review the full-width effect first.
