Heat Transfer Curtain Printing: How Disperse Process Works on Polyester

Heat transfer (disperse printing) is the main process for polyester curtains. J.LINK uses heat transfer for saturated, sun-resistant, seamless polyester curtains from 1 meter.

首页 > 知识库 > Heat Transfer Curtain Printing: How Disperse Process Works on Polyester

Heat transfer (also called disperse printing) is one of the most mature processes for polyester curtain printing. The design is first printed on transfer paper, then high heat turns the disperse ink into gas that penetrates polyester fibers — saturated color, sun and wash resistant, the top choice for curtains, blackout drapes and flags.

How Heat Transfer Works

Heat transfer has two steps: first, wide-format digital printing jets the design onto transfer paper; second, the paper faces the polyester fabric and passes a high-temperature roller, where disperse dye sublimates into gas and penetrates the fibers to fix. The fabric never goes through a wash bath, so wastewater is low.

  • Ink: disperse dye ink for polyester and synthetic
  • Temperature: usually around 200℃ transfer
  • Fixing: gas penetration, stable fastness

  • Pure polyester curtain cloth: most vivid, best sun resistance

Notes

  • Pretreatment: desize/size polyester for even transfer, no bleed
  • Edge: cut edge may show slight white; leave margin when cutting
  • Setting: set after transfer to control shrinkage and drape
  • Sampling: free sampling and sealed sample before bulk is a must
