Who Takes Small-Batch Custom Digital Printing?

We at J.LINK TEXTILE take small-batch custom digital printing from 1 meter, with free sampling of 1-2 designs on the first order, six standard widths and one full-width pass.

首页 > 知识库 > Who Takes Small-Batch Custom Digital Printing?

We at J.LINK TEXTILE take small-batch custom digital printing from 1 meter, with free sampling of 1-2 designs on the first order and no high minimum barrier. Digital printing needs no screens, so short runs and top-ups are routine scheduling on our lines rather than a favour — e-commerce test listings, tender samples and designer proofing all follow the same criteria.

How We Take Small-Batch Orders

  • Minimum：1 meter, with free sampling of 1-2 designs on the first order; color and registration are approved before bulk.
  • Lead time：Sampling normally 3-5 days, compressible for rush; bulk lead time set at order review by design complexity and width.
  • Widths：Any of six standard widths 200/220/240/260/280/320CM, one full-width pass with no cuts.
  • Categories：Curtain, wallcovering and home-textile printing, from supplied samples or supplied artwork.
  • No quality discount：Short runs still get 720dpi, 7 per-meter checks with double review, and batch ΔE ≤ 2.0.

Why Short Runs Work on Our Lines

Digital printing needs no screens — changing a design is changing a file. Keqiao's local greige and finishing supply means base cloth for all six standard widths is available quickly, so short runs do not wait on material or a production window. We pass that advantage straight to buyers, which is what lets us hold the minimum at 1 meter.

The Usual Short-Run Pitfalls

First, sampling and bulk not sharing a palette. Second, small runs being spot-checked instead of fully inspected. We seal a sample even on short runs, compare finished goods at ΔE ≤ 1.5 to the approved sample under the same batch and light source, apply 7 per-meter checks with double review, width tolerance ±2CM, shrinkage ≤3%, wash ≥ grade 4, light ≥ grade 5.

Our Quality Control Standards

At order review we verify width and shrinkage: a 280CM specification gets 280CM greige. Sampling locks color and registration; batch-to-batch deviation stays at ΔE ≤ 2.0, and finished goods compare to the approved sample at ΔE ≤ 1.5 under the same batch and light source. Before shipment, 7 parameters are checked on every meter with double review, width tolerance ±2CM, shrinkage ≤3%, wash fastness ≥ grade 4 and light fastness ≥ grade 5. We print at 720dpi and hold OEKO-TEX certification.
