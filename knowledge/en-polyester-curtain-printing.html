Polyester Curtain Printing: Fabric & Process Matching, Pretreatment

Polyester is the main substrate for curtain printing. J.LINK uses disperse/heat transfer for polyester curtains, wide-format digital printing seamless, 1-meter minimum.

首页 > 知识库 > Polyester Curtain Printing: Fabric & Process Matching, Pretreatment

Polyester is the largest, most cost-effective substrate in curtain printing. It is crisp, sun-resistant and easy care, and with disperse printing and heat transfer yields vivid color. But polyester is not print-ready off the shelf; fabric-process matching and pretreatment decide fastness and hand feel.

How to Choose Process for Polyester Curtains

Two main routes, chosen by need:

  • Disperse / heat transfer: saturated color, smooth gradient, UV resistant, fit for large motifs, photo curtains and south/west windows
  • Wide-format digital printing (direct): seamless full width, fit for floor curtains and blackout drapes

Know the Composition

  • Pure polyester: best for heat transfer, top color and sun
  • Polyester blend: drape and color balanced, pick by main composition
  • Blackout polyester base: heat transfer + coating, control temp to protect coat
  • Synthetic voile: light fabric transfers too, watch even penetration

Pretreatment Notes

Bad pretreatment wastes everything after:

  • Desize/size: remove weaving size for even ink, no bleed
  • GSM tuning: different weights need different jet params
  • Dark brightening: pre-whiten dark fabric to avoid dull print
  • Clean surface: grease/dust free for tight transfer

Common Myths

  • All synthetics use one process: composition differs, disperse/heat params differ
  • Skip pretreatment causes bleed and color spots
  • No sampling before bulk means high color risk

常见问题

Which process for polyester curtains?

Pure polyester uses disperse/heat transfer for saturated, sun-resistant color; large floor curtains use wide-format digital printing seamless.

Pretreatment needed for polyester?

Yes: desize, GSM tuning, dark brightening and clean surface avoid bleed and dull color.

Process for polyester blend curtains?

By main composition: polyester-led uses heat transfer; for drape and color balance use wide-format direct, confirm composition first.
