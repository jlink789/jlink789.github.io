Direct Wallcovering Printing: Pigment vs Disperse Direct — Principle, Advantage, Use, Caution

Direct printing for wallcoverings jets ink straight onto the base fabric, skipping the screen-making step of traditional printing. By base composition and ink system, the mainstream splits into pigment direct printing and disperse direct printing, with different principles and suited scenes.

What Is Direct Wallcovering Printing

Direct printing uses digital heads to jet ink onto fabric by the design, then fixes color so it stays. No screens means the design file goes on machine immediately — ideal for small-batch, multi-design wallcovering orders. J.LINK TEXTILE's wide-format digital printing covers six widths 200-320CM, up to 320CM, printed in one piece for a seamless wall.

Pigment vs Disperse Direct

ItemPigment directDisperse direct
BasePolyester, synthetic, blendMainly polyester, synthetic
PrincipleBinder fixes ink on fiber surfaceHigh-temp transfer penetrates polyester
HandSlightly stiff, firmSoft, close to greige
ColorMedium, fastness by fixingSaturated, smooth gradients
FlowPrint+fix, shortPrint+heat transfer, needs sublimation step
Best forSmall batch, fast, mixedBulk, vivid, sun-resistant

Advantages of Direct Printing

Flexibility is the biggest win: no screens means 1-meter minimum and free sampling are routine; a new design samples in 1-2 days, letting brands test cheaply. Second, full-width power: wide-format direct at 280-320CM prints one wall in a single piece with continuous pattern.

Color: 8 inks, 16.7M colors with ICC curves hold batch ΔE under 1.5, sample equals bulk. Direct printing is also IP-friendly — the digital file is the design, so original and custom-supplied work is clearly traceable, lowering infringement risk.

Use Cases

  • Living-room feature walls, bedroom: small motifs, plain, textures
  • Designer custom designs: multi-SKU, small-batch fast response
  • Project continuous rolls: uniform color, continuous
  • Sun-exposed spaces: disperse direct for saturation and sun resistance

Cautions

Match base: pigment direct is broadly compatible but firmer; disperse direct suits polyester/synthetic — confirm composition first. Confirm fixing: pigment fastness depends on fixing; sample before bulk to confirm grade 4+ fastness. Plan width: large walls prefer 280-320CM in one piece to keep pattern continuous.
