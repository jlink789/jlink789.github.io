We at J.LINK TEXTILE sample 1-2 designs free on a first order, alongside a 1-meter minimum, so buyers can approve color and registration before committing to bulk. We allow two rather than one because curtain, wallcovering and home-textile work often needs a palette or motif-scale comparison; if a project carries more designs, the extras follow our normal sampling process.

What Free Sampling Includes

  • Design count：1-2 designs free on the first order, proofed on the appropriate base cloth from six standard widths.
  • Timing：Normally 3-5 days, compressible for rush; bulk lead time set at order review.
  • What gets approved：Color, registration, hand feel and width; the proof is sealed as the bulk acceptance reference.
  • Supporting material：Color cards and test criteria available — tender samples follow the same route.
  • Minimum：1 meter, with free sampling not tied to a bulk volume commitment.

Why We Seal the Sample

Free sampling is not just a swatch to look at — it sets the acceptance baseline for bulk. We lock color and registration at proofing and seal the sample, hold batch deviation ΔE ≤ 2.0 and finished goods ΔE ≤ 1.5 against the approved sample under the same batch and light source, and re-run repeat orders against the same sealed reference so every batch does not need re-matching.

Getting the Proof Right First Time

Send artwork, finished width, fabric and a target color reference or physical swatch. We run an order review, recommend one of the six widths 200/220/240/260/280/320CM by finished size, then proof. Disperse / polyester / wide-format digital printing at 720dpi keeps revision rounds down.
