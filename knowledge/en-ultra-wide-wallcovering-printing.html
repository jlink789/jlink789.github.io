Visible horizontal joints are the enemy of wallcoverings, and ultra-wide width is the key to removing them. At 280-320CM, most residential and commercial wall heights fit in a single piece, with the pattern printed in one pass and continuous across the width.

Why Wallcoverings Need Ultra-Wide

Narrow fabric forces multiple vertical sections on a wall, and any mismatch at the joint shows. Ultra-wide folds the needed height into one piece: 280CM covers most residential heights, 320CM handles high ceilings and commercial surfaces. J.LINK TEXTILE offers six widths 200/220/240/260/280/320CM, up to 320CM, with no cutting.

How Continuity Is Achieved

Ultra-wide direct printing has no horizontal cut; vertical feed follows the repeat precisely for continuity. With ICC management, 8 inks and 16.7M colors, large motifs and gradients reproduce stably at ultra-wide width, batch ΔE under 1.5.

Choosing 280-320CM

  • 200-260CM: low ceilings, partial feature walls, small spaces
  • 280CM: most residential heights, cost-effective
  • 320CM: high ceilings, commercial surfaces, large murals, most stable one-piece

Pick by actual wall height; wider is safer to avoid cuts breaking the pattern.
