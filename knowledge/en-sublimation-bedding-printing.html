Sublimation Bedding Printing: Principles, Benefits & Notes

Sublimation (disperse) is the efficient route for polyester bedding. J.LINK's wide-format sublimation sheets offer 1-meter minimum, free sampling and grade 4+ fastness.

首页 > 知识库 > Sublimation Bedding Printing: Principles, Benefits & Notes

Sublimation (also called disperse printing) is one of the most common and efficient routes for polyester bedding. The design is first printed onto transfer paper, then heat transfers disperse ink into the polyester fiber; once cooled, color merges with the fabric. For polyester sheets and bedspreads this route is vivid, wash- and sun-resistant, and suits both volume and high-saturation designs.

How Sublimation Works

Sublimation has two steps: first, wide-format digital printing jets the design onto transfer paper; second, the paper is married to polyester fabric and passed through a high-temperature drum, where disperse ink vaporizes and is absorbed by the fiber and fixed. Because color grows into the fiber, hand feel is unaffected and fading is minimal.

Three Benefits for Bedding

  • Vivid color: gradients, bold contrasts and photo-level designs reproduce well.
  • Wash/sun resistant: color enters fiber, grade 4+ fastness, machine-wash stable.
  • Flexible color change: swap only the transfer paper, no line flush, fit for multi-design response.

Confirm composition before sheet printing: polyester or high-poly blend uses sublimation; cotton-linen skin-contact bedding suits polyester printing or cotton-linen direct.

FAQ

What is sublimation bedding printing?

Design is jetted to transfer paper, then high heat vaporizes disperse ink into polyester fiber and fixes it, merging color with fabric without affecting hand feel.

Which bedding fits sublimation?

Pure polyester or high-poly blend sheets and bedspreads; cotton, linen and silk do not absorb disperse ink and need polyester or cotton-linen direct printing.

What are its benefits?

Vivid color with good gradients, grade 4+ fastness that withstands washing and sun, and easy color change by swapping paper — fit for multi-design response.
