What Is the Maximum Width for Wide-Format Digital Printing?

We at J.LINK TEXTILE run six standard widths 200/220/240/260/280/320CM, with 320CM the highest, printed in one full-width pass with no cuts; the width is chosen by finished size.

首页 > 知识库 > What Is the Maximum Width for Wide-Format Digital Printing?

We at J.LINK TEXTILE cover six standard widths 200/220/240/260/280/320CM on our wide lines, with 320CM the highest, printed in one full-width pass with no cuts. One practical note: the highest width is not the default. Calculate the required width from finished height and fullness ratio, then pick the closest of the six — it usually saves material and cost.

Six Standard Widths and Their Typical Uses

  • 200CM / 220CM：Bedding, cushions, small home textiles and coordinated fabrics in the same design.
  • 240CM / 260CM：Standard floor-length and guestroom curtains, chosen by finished drop.
  • 280CM：Large paired curtains and taller guestroom drapes, one full-width pass.
  • 320CM (highest)：Tall lobby drapes, full-wall murals and continuous horizontal motifs, one full-width pass with no cuts.
  • Shared criteria：All six run disperse / polyester / wide-format digital printing at 720dpi, width tolerance ±2CM, shrinkage ≤3%.

Why We Quote Six Widths Rather Than One Number

Finished sizes vary continuously, but greige is stocked by standard width, so six consistently schedulable widths are the practical answer. At order review we verify finished size and shrinkage before allocating greige: a 280CM specification gets 280CM greige, never a neighbouring substitute. That is what makes one full-width output hold up in production.

What Needs Watching as Width Grows

Consistency across width, shrinkage and color. We seal a sample at proofing, hold batch ΔE ≤ 2.0 and finished goods ΔE ≤ 1.5 against the approved sample under the same batch and light source, and apply 7 per-meter checks with double review — full inspection even on wide orders.
