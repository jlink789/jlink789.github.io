320CM Ultra-Wide Curtain Printing: One-Piece Value for Floor Drapes

320CM ultra-wide makes floor curtains one-piece and uncut. J.LINK wide-format digital printing up to 320CM, 1-meter minimum, Keqiao digital printing factory direct.

首页 > 知识库 > 320CM Ultra-Wide Curtain Printing: One-Piece Value for Floor Drapes

Floor curtains are often 2.8-3.2m tall; ordinary narrow machines cannot print the full height and must split into two. The value of 320CM ultra-wide is to print a floor curtain in one piece, uncut, lifting both visual and quality.

Why Ultra-Wide for Curtains

Floor curtains often exceed ordinary width in height. Narrow width forces horizontal splitting, costing labor and breaking pattern continuity. 320CM wide-format direct printing completes the whole cloth at once:

  • Continuous pattern: large motifs and landscapes span uncut
  • Complete visual: even drape, no horizontal seam
  • Fewer steps: no aligning/joining, faster delivery
  • Higher yield: one less join, fewer defects
