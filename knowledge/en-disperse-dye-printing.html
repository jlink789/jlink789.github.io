What Are Disperse & Polyester Printing? How to Choose the 3 Processes

Disperse and polyester printing are the two core processes of wide-format digital printing, for polyester/synthetic and home-textile blends respectively.

首页 > 知识库 > What Are Disperse & Polyester Printing? How to Choose the 3 Processes

Disperse and polyester printing are the two core processes of J.LINK's wide-format line, together with wide-format digital printing forming three processes. Choosing right decides saturation, sun resistance and fastness.

Disperse Printing

Disperse printing uses disperse dyes for polyester and synthetic fabric. Dyes migrate into fibers under heat, giving vivid, wash- and sun-resistant color—first choice for curtains and synthetic wallcoverings, across all six standard widths.

Polyester Printing

Polyester printing broadly covers polyester fabrics; in home textiles it often means soft, skin-friendly printing of polyester blends, fit for bedding and cushions.

Wide-Format Digital Printing

Wide-format digital printing is the capability of jetting on 200-320CM full width, carrying disperse or polyester inks, seamless. It is more a equipment capability combined with the former two.

How to Choose

  • Curtains, synthetic wallcovering → disperse
  • Bedding, cushions → polyester
  • Floor-length drapes, feature walls → wide-format (carrying matching ink)

常见问题

Disperse vs polyester printing?

Disperse dyes target polyester/synthetic, vivid and sun-resistant; polyester printing means soft blends for home textiles.

Which fabric uses disperse?

Polyester and synthetic; heat-set, wash- and sun-resistant, first choice for curtains and synthetic wallcovering.

Is wide-format a third process?

It is a full-width jetting capability carrying disperse or polyester inks—combined with the former two for 200-320CM seamless.

Wrong process consequence?

Color deviation, low fastness or wrong hand feel; free sampling first is advised.
