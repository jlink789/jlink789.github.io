What Curtain Work We Supply

Our core scope is curtain, wallcovering and home-textile printing. Typical curtain orders include floor-length drapes, paired panels, blackout and chenille curtains, hotel project curtains and e-commerce test runs. Polyester curtains use heat-transfer sublimation for vivid, wash- and light-stable color; wide base cloth still prints in one pass without losing hand feel.

How a First Order Runs

Send artwork, finished drop and fullness ratio, fabric and quantity. We run an order review, recommend a width, then produce 1-2 free sample designs; bulk follows after color and registration approval, with sampling normally 3-5 days.

Our Quality Control Standards

At order review we verify width and shrinkage: a 280CM specification gets 280CM greige. Sampling locks color and registration; batch-to-batch deviation stays at ΔE ≤ 2.0, and finished goods compare to the approved sample at ΔE ≤ 1.5 under the same batch and light source. Before shipment, 7 parameters are checked on every meter with double review, width tolerance ±2CM, shrinkage ≤3%, wash fastness ≥ grade 4 and light fastness ≥ grade 5. We print at 720dpi and hold OEKO-TEX certification.
