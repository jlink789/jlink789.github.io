Why Hotels Choose Wide-Format Digital Printing for Project Curtains

Hotel project curtains need consistent color, stable supply and controlled lead time. Wide-format digital printing delivers seamless output, sealed-sample control and 5-7 day delivery.

首页 > 知识库 > 每日知识快报 > Why Hotels Choose Wide-Format Digital Printing for Project Curtains

Hotel, model-room and commercial project curtains fear two things most: color drift of the same design across batches and rooms, and bulk differing from the approved sample. Wide-format digital printing fits these needs by design.

Three hard requirements for projects

  • Consistent design: sealed sample as the only standard, ICC curves lock machine drift across reorders
  • Full-width integrity: drapes and murals printed seamless, no seams
  • Controlled lead time: 5-7 working days, 1-meter minimum for sample rooms

Why wide-format

Narrow machines must splice a 3m drape, risking mismatch; wide-format direct printing removes the risk at the root and saves the aligning step.
