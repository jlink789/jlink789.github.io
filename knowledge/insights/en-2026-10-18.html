How Cross-Border Sellers Choose a Digital Printing Factory: 4 Checks

Cross-border sellers compete on speed: new launches and replenishment. Check four things: wide-format, small-batch response, export compliance, stable lead time.

首页 > 知识库 > 每日知识快报 > How Cross-Border Sellers Choose a Digital Printing Factory: 4 Checks

Cross-border soft-furnishing competes on speed: fast launches, fast replenishment, low inventory risk. When choosing a digital printing factory, don't只看 price — check four hard metrics.

Four must-checks

  • Wide-format: curtains and wallcoverings often need 200-320CM seamless; narrow splicing hurts quality
  • Small-batch response: 1-meter minimum, free sampling, zero-pressure trials
  • Export compliance: OEKO-TEX, SGS support, eco-ink standards
  • Stable lead time: 5-7 working days with consistent reorders
