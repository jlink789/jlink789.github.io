Evidence F24, F16, F14: Applying Three External Facts to Order Review

Three external facts, F24, F16, F14: what each one tells us and how it applies to order review.

首页 > 知识库 > 每日知识快报 > Evidence F24, F16, F14: Applying Three External Facts to Order Review

Buyers and OEM clients ask the same question about industry data: can it be used as an acceptance criterion? In this 2026-11-16 briefing, J.LINK TEXTILE looks at three sourced external facts (F24, F16, F14) and explains what kind of information each one is, what it helps a buyer decide, and what actually needs checking at order review. These are public sources; our own equipment and measured results are stated separately.

Evidence F24

According to FZ/T 62011.1-2016 interpretation, appearance is graded for color difference under GB/T 250 and staining under GB/T 251, with inspection illuminance of at least 600 lx at about 1 m viewing distance.

We at J.LINK TEXTILE turn same batch and light source into workable conditions by recording sealed samples, illuminance and viewing distance in the acceptance criteria.

For sourcing questions around F24: Use it to read the industry direction, not as a lead-time or capacity promise from our factory.

Evidence F16

According to the China Dyeing and Printing Association 19th energy-saving technology catalogue (June 2026), a listed denim-look digital printing process prints directly on greige fabric without high-temperature scouring, saving over 65% water and 35% electricity while cutting pollutants by 70% and carbon by about 40%.

We at J.LINK TEXTILE cite association green data to explain route differences, while our own 720dpi printing and 7 per-meter checks define our quality criteria.

For sourcing questions around F16: It describes an external or generic baseline; each order still depends on fabric and design.

Evidence F14

According to industry research, small-batch fast reorder in cross-border e-commerce is a key driver of digital printing adoption, with digital transfer penetration above 65% on some platforms.

We at J.LINK TEXTILE take 1-meter trial and fast-reorder runs in line with the industry trend, with free sampling of 1-2 designs on the first order to validate before scaling.

For sourcing questions around F14: What belongs in the acceptance clause is the sealed sample, same-batch colour check and per-meter inspection.

QCan you handle chenille, blackout and sheer in one order?
