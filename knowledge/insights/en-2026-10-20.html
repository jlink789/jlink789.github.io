Root Causes of Color Complaints and Fixes: Seal the Sample Before Talking Color

Bulk differing from sample usually comes from no sealed sample or machine drift. The fix: sealed sample as the only standard, ICC curves to lock drift, standardized fixing.

首页 > 知识库 > 每日知识快报 > Root Causes of Color Complaints and Fixes: Seal the Sample Before Talking Color

Bulk differing from the approved sample is the top complaint in printing OEM, but the root cause almost always falls into two types: no sealed sample, or uncontrolled machine drift.

Root-cause breakdown

  • No seal: no single reference, bulk judged by eye, subjective and drifting
  • Machine drift: different machines/batches with inconsistent curves output different colors
  • Random fixing: varying steaming/washing shifts color later

Three-step fix

1. Seal the sample: first piece or supplied sample sealed as the only bulk standard

2. Curve lock: independent ICC curve per line for cross-machine consistency

3. Standardized fixing: fixed steaming/washing/finishing, no color shift

Done well, batch ΔE stays under 1.5 — sample equals bulk.
