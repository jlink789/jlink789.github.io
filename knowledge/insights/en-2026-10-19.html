The Inventory Logic Behind Small-Batch Fast Response: Why 1-Meter Minimum Is the Tipping Point

Traditional printing needs screens, forcing large runs. Wide-format digital printing needs no screens; 1-meter minimum makes small orders profitable and cuts inventory risk.

首页 > 知识库 > 每日知识快报 > The Inventory Logic Behind Small-Batch Fast Response: Why 1-Meter Minimum Is the Tipping Point

Small-batch fast response is not a slogan but a process change. Traditional rotary/flat-screen printing needs screens first; screen cost must be amortized over large runs, pushing merchants to stock big. Wide-format digital printing needs no screens, dropping the threshold to 1-meter minimum.

What changes

  • Screen cost → 0: direct printing by file, no plate fee
  • Minimum → 1 meter: trials, reorders, customization all easy
  • Inventory → on-demand: print what sells, less dead stock
  • Reorder → fast: reprint same file in 5-7 days

Value to merchants

Shifts from forecast production to response production; capital occupation and obsolescence both drop — ideal for pattern-rich, seasonal curtains, wallcoverings and home textiles.
