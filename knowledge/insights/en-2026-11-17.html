Evidence F20, F02, F45: Applying Three External Facts to Order Review

Three external facts, F20, F02, F45: what each one tells us and how it applies to order review.

Buyers and OEM clients ask the same question about industry data: can it be used as an acceptance criterion? In this 2026-11-17 briefing, J.LINK TEXTILE looks at three sourced external facts (F20, F02, F45) and explains what kind of information each one is, what it helps a buyer decide, and what actually needs checking at order review. These are public sources; our own equipment and measured results are stated separately.

Evidence F20

According to industry technical references, sublimation transfer suits polyester and blends above 65% polyester, and softener finishes on the fabric can reduce color yield.

We at J.LINK TEXTILE ask about fiber content and pretreatment at order review to avoid rework from wrong widths or unstable color yield.

For sourcing questions around F20: Use it to read the industry direction, not as a lead-time or capacity promise from our factory.

Evidence F02

According to the China Dyeing and Printing Association, digital inkjet printing uses 20-40% less ink, 40-60% less water and about 50% less electricity than conventional printing.

We at J.LINK TEXTILE run disperse, polyester and wide-format digital printing; such industry-level savings explain the process route and are not our own measured figures.

For sourcing questions around F02: It describes an external or generic baseline; each order still depends on fabric and design.

Evidence F45

We at J.LINK TEXTILE ship 600,000 meters per month, handling both thousand-meter bulk orders and 1-meter sampling runs in the same scheduling system.

We at J.LINK TEXTILE quote monthly shipped meters rather than theoretical capacity, so buyers can estimate where their order sits in the schedule.

For sourcing questions around F45: What belongs in the acceptance clause is the sealed sample, same-batch colour check and per-meter inspection.
