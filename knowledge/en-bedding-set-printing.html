Bedding Set Printing: Fabric Matching & Process for Sheets

Among a 4-piece bedding set, the flat sheet is the largest and most visible. J.LINK matches polyester/disperse/wide-format processes, with 1-meter minimum and free sampling.

首页 > 知识库 > Bedding Set Printing: Fabric Matching & Process for Sheets

A 4-piece bedding set usually includes a flat sheet, duvet cover, pillowcases and a fitted sheet. The flat sheet is the largest and most eye-catching piece, so sheet printing is often the visual core of the whole set. Get the sheet's fabric and process right first, then extend the style to the duvet and pillowcases for consistent quality.

Why Set the Sheet First

The sheet is used most and covers the largest area; its design, color and hand decide the first impression of the whole set. Once the sheet printing is set, covers and pillowcases follow with the same or coordinating design, lowering development cost and stabilizing reorders.

  • High visual weight: the sheet covers over half the bed; clarity and pattern match are instantly visible.
  • Process baseline: once the sheet uses polyester or disperse, matching pieces share the process for easier color control.
  • Width baseline: oversized sheets decide whether wide-format seamless printing is needed.

Skin-contact sheets use polyester printing for softness; bold decor uses disperse for vividness; sheets wider than standard use wide-format digital printing for one-piece output.

Standard Workflow

1. Confirm fabric and set size, set the sheet design.

2. Free sampling in 1-2 days, check color and pattern match.

3. Seal the sample as the only bulk standard.

4. Bulk by wide-format direct printing, seamless.

5. QC and shipment: ΔE<1.5, pattern match, width check.

Common Mistakes

  • Mixing processes across the set breaks color consistency.
  • Oversized sheets on narrow width show mismatched repeats.
  • Skipping sampling risks the costliest color complaints on large sheets.
  • Ignoring fastness: skin-contact bedding needs grade 4+.
