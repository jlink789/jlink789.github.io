How to Choose a Curtain Printing Factory: Five-Dimension View

Choosing a curtain printing factory is more than price. J.LINK evaluates by width, process, design, lead time and service, with wide-format sublimation printing, 1-meter minimum.

首页 > 知识库 > How to Choose a Curtain Printing Factory: Five-Dimension View

Curtains are the most typical wide-format product: a floor-length drape is 2.8-3.2m tall and must be seamless. Many curtain printing factories exist, but "can print" and "prints stably and delivers on time" are different. Evaluation must go beyond per-meter price across five dimensions.

Dimension 1: Width & Capacity (seamless?)

Curtains fear mismatched repeats from narrow splits. Prefer factories covering six standard widths 200-320CM up to 320CM, printing oversized drapes in one piece. Also check daily capacity and peak stability.

Dimension 2: Process Maturity

Polyester curtains commonly use sublimation (disperse); some blends use polyester printing. Ask for grade 4+ fastness and batch ΔE<1.5; factories with OEKO-TEX/SGS verification are safer.

Dimension 3: Design & IP

Curtain designs refresh fast. Ask about the original design library size, monthly updates, private-mold cycle and complete license chain to avoid infringement.

Dimension 4: Lead Time & Elasticity

E-commerce and cross-border curtain orders fluctuate; whether digital printing supports 1-meter minimum, free sampling and 5-7 day lead time, with fast small-batch response, decides replenishment rhythm. Sampling in 1-2 days is a plus.

Dimension 5: Service & Compliance

Reconciliation transparency, loss accounting, invoice type and after-sales response should be confirmed upfront. Put defect handling and loss accounting in the contract.
