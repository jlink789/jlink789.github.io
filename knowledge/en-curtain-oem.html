Curtain Printing OEM: From Sampling to Bulk Production

Curtains need seamless full-width printing. J.LINK offers 1-meter minimum, free sampling and 5-7 day delivery with no seams.

首页 > 知识库 > Curtain Printing OEM: From Sampling to Bulk Production

Curtains are the most typical wide-format case: a floor-to-ceiling drape is 2.8-3.2m tall and must be seamless. A standardized OEM workflow directly controls color and lead-time risk.

Standard OEM Flow

1. Confirm width (200-320CM), fabric (polyester/synthetic), design file

2. Free sampling in 1-2 days, confirm color and pattern match

3. Seal the sample as the bulk standard

4. Bulk production by wide-format direct printing, seamless

5. QC and shipment: delta E, pattern match, width check

Why Seamless Matters

Seams on drapes show mismatched patterns instantly. Wide-format direct printing avoids all seam defects and the aligning step.
