How We Keep Wide-Format Output Consistent

The real risk on wide orders is a good sample and a drifting bulk run. We verify width and shrinkage at order review — a 280CM specification gets 280CM greige, never a narrower substitute. Sampling locks color and registration; batch-to-batch deviation stays at ΔE ≤ 2.0, and finished goods compare to the approved sample at ΔE ≤ 1.5 under the same batch and light source. Before shipment, 7 parameters are checked on every meter by machine plus human visual inspection, double-reviewed rather than spot-checked.
