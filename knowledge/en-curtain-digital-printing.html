What Is Curtain Digital Printing? Seamless Curtain Customization

Curtain digital printing jets patterns onto 200-320CM full-width curtain fabric, seamless, 1-meter minimum, 5-7 day delivery.

首页 > 知识库 > What Is Curtain Digital Printing? Seamless Curtain Customization

Curtain digital printing uses wide-format machines to jet patterns onto 200-320CM full-width curtain fabric. Versus narrow joined panels, it is seamless, avoiding mismatch and seams, ideal for blackout, sheers and floor-length drapes.

Why Curtains Fit Digital

Curtains are large vertical surfaces where seams and mismatch are most visible. Wide-format prints in one piece with continuous pattern. With 1-meter minimum and free sampling, trials, replenishment and fast response are efficient.
