How Does One-Piece Full-Width Printing Differ From Sectioned Panels?

We at J.LINK TEXTILE print one full-width pass with no cuts across six standard widths, so the finished panel is continuous and precisely matched, with the later registration work removed entirely.

首页 > 知识库 > How Does One-Piece Full-Width Printing Differ From Sectioned Panels?

We at J.LINK TEXTILE print in one full-width pass with no cuts: pick the required width from six standards 200/220/240/260/280/320CM and print it in one go. The difference is less about sharpness than about whether the finished panel is one continuous piece — our approach keeps it uninterrupted and precisely matched, with no downstream registration or joining step.

Four Practical Differences of One-Piece Full Width

  • Continuous result：One panel from end to end, precisely matched, so continuous horizontal motifs and gradients stay intact.
  • Fewer steps：No later registration or joining, so the finishing chain is shorter and lead time more predictable.
  • Stable dimensions：Full-width greige with width tolerance ±2CM and shrinkage ≤3% keeps finished sizing easy to calculate.
  • Uniform color：One output pass keeps the palette consistent: batch ΔE ≤ 2.0, finished goods ΔE ≤ 1.5 against the approved sample.
  • Flexible width：Six standard widths chosen by finished size — no need to push every job to 320CM.

Where the Difference Shows Most

Tall hotel lobby drapes, paired curtains in large apartments, full-wall mural wallcovering, and designs with continuous horizontal motifs or broad gradients benefit most. We serve 500+ B2B buyers and OEM clients, and project and export orders give the clearest feedback: once the panel is hung, a single uninterrupted width is visible at a glance.

How We Keep a Full-Width Pass Stable

Width alone is not enough — width, shrinkage and color have to be controlled together. We verify width and shrinkage before allocating greige, run disperse / polyester / wide-format digital printing at 720dpi, seal a sample at proofing, then apply 7 per-meter checks with double review, wash ≥ grade 4, light ≥ grade 5, OEKO-TEX certified.
