What Is Wallcovering Digital Printing? Seamless Mural-Grade Walls

Wallcovering digital printing jets onto 200-320CM full-width wall fabric for seamless murals, sun-resistant and durable, 1-meter minimum.

首页 > 知识库 > What Is Wallcovering Digital Printing? Seamless Mural-Grade Walls

Wallcovering digital printing jets patterns onto full-width wall fabric for seamless mural-grade results. Versus joined panels, it is continuous with no seam, ideal for large feature walls and hotel projects.

Why Wallcovering Fits Digital

Wallcovering is a continuous large surface; seams break the picture. Wide-format prints in one piece, continuous and seamless, with sun-resistant inks that stay vivid.
