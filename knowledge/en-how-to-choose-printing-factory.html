How to Choose a Digital Printing Factory? 6 Key Questions to Ask

Choose by width, process, minimum, lead time, QC and capacity. J.LINK TEXTILE answers with 200-320CM widths, 1-meter minimum and 5-7 day delivery.

首页 > 知识库 > How to Choose a Digital Printing Factory? 6 Key Questions to Ask

Choosing a digital printing factory is not only about unit price, but whether it matches your width, process, minimum and lead-time needs. Here are 6 key questions and how J.LINK TEXTILE answers them.

1. Is the Width Enough?

Ask the maximum width and standard tiers. J.LINK covers six widths 200/220/240/260/280/320CM, up to 320CM, seamless—no joining for large curtains and wallcoverings.

2. Are Processes Complete?

Different fabrics need different processes. J.LINK has disperse, polyester and wide-format digital printing, covering polyester, synthetic and blends.

3. How High Is the Minimum?

For trial and replenishment, minimum is a hard gate. J.LINK offers 1-meter minimum and free sampling (1-2 designs first order), sampling fee deductible on bulk.

4. Is Lead Time Stable?

J.LINK standard bulk is 5-7 working days, sampling 1-2 days, rush negotiable; 8+ lines run 24 hours.

5. How Is QC & Fastness?

Ask for evidence. J.LINK uses three-stage QC, grade 4+ fastness, ΔE<1.5, with test reports available.

6. Can Bulk Capacity Keep Up?

Small factories can't take bulk; large ones reject small. J.LINK makes 600k+ meters monthly, both 1-meter sampling and thousand-meter bulk.

常见问题

What to check first?

Width and process fit. For large curtains/wallcoverings check 200-320CM seamless; match fabric to disperse/polyester/wide-format process.

Is lower minimum always better?

For trials yes, but also check bulk ability. Ideal is like J.LINK: 1-meter minimum yet stable thousand-meter bulk.

How to verify QC?

Ask for fastness and color evidence: grade 4+, ΔE<1.5, with test reports and three-stage QC.

What lead time is normal?

Digital standard bulk 5-7 days, sampling 1-2 days is normal fast-response; much longer signals capacity issues.
