Verifiable Details of Our 320CM Line

  • 320CM is routine：All six standard widths 200/220/240/260/280/320CM are scheduled in-house; 320CM is the flagship, not a rare one-off run.
  • One full-width pass：320CM is printed in one full-width pass with no cuts — floor-length drapes, lobby curtains and mural wallcovering stay continuous.
  • Wide base cloth on hand：Greige and finishing cluster locally in Keqiao, so 320CM base cloth is available the same day and revisions do not wait on material.
  • Stable dimensions：Width tolerance ±2CM, shrinkage ≤3%; width and shrinkage are verified before greige is allocated.
  • Full inspection：7 checks on every meter, double-reviewed, machine detection plus human per-meter visual inspection, records available with the goods.

Why 'Can Do' and 'Can Do Consistently' Differ

The real cost of wide-format work is repeatability: one good run is easy; the second and third must hold the same width, color and shrinkage. We seal a sample at proofing, hold batch deviation at ΔE ≤ 2.0 and finished goods at ΔE ≤ 1.5 against the approved sample under the same batch and light source, with 720dpi output and per-meter inspection.

Which Orders Actually Need 320CM

Tall lobby drapes, paired curtains in large apartments and full-wall mural wallcovering need one continuous piece, so 320CM fits. Where finished drop and fullness ratio work out to 260CM or 280CM, we recommend that width instead — it saves material and cost without pushing every order to the flagship width.

Our Quality Control Standards

At order review we verify width and shrinkage: a 280CM specification gets 280CM greige. Sampling locks color and registration; batch-to-batch deviation stays at ΔE ≤ 2.0, and finished goods compare to the approved sample at ΔE ≤ 1.5 under the same batch and light source. Before shipment, 7 parameters are checked on every meter with double review, width tolerance ±2CM, shrinkage ≤3%, wash fastness ≥ grade 4 and light fastness ≥ grade 5. We print at 720dpi and hold OEKO-TEX certification.
